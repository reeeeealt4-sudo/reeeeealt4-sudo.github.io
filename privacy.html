Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Who this applies to

Shorts Pipeline ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its operator to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the operator's YouTube channels, used solely to upload videos and read the connected channel's name.
  • Public data from the YouTube Data API (titles of publicly listed videos) to research trending topics.

How data is stored and shared

All tokens and data stay on the operator's own computer. The app does not run a server, does not collect analytics, and does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. Deleting the app's local token files also removes all stored access.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the "About" page of the connected YouTube channels.
