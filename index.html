Shorts Pipeline

Shorts Pipeline is a private, personal tool that produces original short-form videos (explainers, short fiction, and open-source AI tool roundups) and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts, using the YouTube API Services and the TikTok Content Posting API.

It is not a public service: no one else can sign up, and it only ever accesses accounts belonging to its operator.

Videos produced by this tool use AI-generated narration and imagery and are labeled as altered or synthetic content on upload.
